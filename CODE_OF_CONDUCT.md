# Code of Conduct

VPN Exit Bench welcomes constructive participation from users and contributors.

## Expected behavior

- communicate respectfully and focus criticism on code/behavior, not people;
- provide reproducible technical information where possible;
- respect privacy and never publish another person's credentials, private keys or identifying network data;
- avoid harassment, threats, discrimination and deliberately disruptive behavior;
- follow the security-reporting process instead of publicly disclosing an unpatched vulnerability.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, personal attacks, doxxing, deliberate disclosure of secrets, spam, malicious payloads presented as normal test data, or attempts to exploit community members are not acceptable.

## Scope

This applies to repository issues, pull requests, discussions and other project-managed community spaces.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit, hide or remove contributions and restrict participation when necessary to protect the project or community. For sensitive conduct concerns, contact the maintainer through the GitHub profile at `https://github.com/mlo-Tek` rather than posting private information publicly.
